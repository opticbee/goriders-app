Frequently Asked Questions

Can I have a lost item delivered to me? ▼
Unfortunately, GoRide doesn't offer a service to deliver lost items. You can contact your driver through the app to arrange a pickup.
Can I rent a car using GoRide? ▼
GoRide doesn't directly offer car rentals. However, they may partner with rental services in some locations.
Can I request a ride that picks up friends in different locations? ▼
You can add multiple destinations to your ride before you request it, to pick up friends at different locations.
Can I request a taxi on GoRide? ▼
In some cities, GoRide offers a taxi option, allowing you to request a traditional taxi through the GoRide app.
Is there a GoRide ride option for 5 people? ▼
Yes, GoRide offers options like GoRideXL or GoRideSUV that can accommodate 5 passengers. Be sure to select the appropriate option when requesting your ride.
