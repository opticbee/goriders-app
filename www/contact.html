Frequently Asked Questions

How do I contact GoRide customer support?

You can contact our 24/7 customer support through the in-app chat, by calling our toll-free number (+91 80 1234 5678), or by filling out the contact form on this page.

What are GoRide's office hours?

Our customer support operates 24/7. Our physical offices are open Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Appointments are recommended for in-person visits.

How can I report a safety concern?

For immediate safety concerns during a ride, use the emergency button in the app. For non-urgent safety reports, please use the "Safety Concern" option in the contact form above or call our dedicated safety hotline at +91 80 9876 5432.

Where can I find GoRide's terms and policies?

All our terms of service, privacy policy, and community guidelines are available in the "Legal" section at the bottom of every page on our website, as well as in the app under Settings > Legal.
