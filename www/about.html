GoRide Story

Revolutionizing urban mobility one ride at a time

Our Mission

At GoRide, we're on a mission to transform urban transportation by making it affordable, reliable, and accessible to everyone. We believe that getting around your city should be as simple as tapping a button, without the hassles of traditional transportation methods.

Our Journey

2025

Founded in Hyderabad with just 10 bikes and a big dream to solve urban mobility challenges

2025

Expanded to 10 cities across India and launched our auto-rickshaw service

2026

Introduced cab services and reached 1 million rides per day milestone

2026

Launched in 150+ cities with over 1 million driver partners

Today

Serving 25M+ customers with 100M+ rides completed across India
